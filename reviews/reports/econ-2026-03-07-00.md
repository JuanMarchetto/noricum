## Executive Summary

**Overall Weighted Score: 4.80 / 10.0 — Grade: D+**

Noricum is a technically impressive solo project with a genuinely differentiated product in a large and growing market. The 9-stage migration pipeline with automated differential testing and LLM-driven repair is unique among existing tools. However, the project has **zero public traction, no revenue path infrastructure, and no community**. The gap between technical capability and economic viability is the defining challenge.

**Bottom line:** The technology works. The product doesn't exist yet (in a market sense). Noricum is a strong engineering prototype that needs aggressive go-to-market execution to become economically viable.
