### Assessment

The technical moat is moderate. The **verification + repair loop** is genuinely differentiated — no other publicly available tool does this. The pipeline architecture is well-designed and would take a competitor weeks to replicate. However:

- **LLM intelligence is not a moat.** Any competitor with Claude/GPT access can build similar translation agents. The prompts are not proprietary enough.

- **Diff testing is the real moat** — but it's architecturally simple. A determined competitor could replicate it in days.
- **15,263 lines of Rust** is substantial engineering but not enough to deter fast followers.

The moat is more about **integration quality** than any single hard-to-replicate capability.

**Score: 6/10** — Solid pipeline architecture with genuine verification differentiator, but individual components are replicable.

### Recommendations

2. Add property-based/fuzz testing to verification (mentioned in docs but not implemented)
